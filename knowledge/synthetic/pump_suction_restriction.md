# Synthetic Engineering Note — Pump Suction-Side Restriction

**Provenance:** Synthetic document written specifically for this portfolio project. It is not copied from an employer, customer, OEM manual, or proprietary failure-mode library.

For a centrifugal pump, increasing differential pressure across a suction strainer can indicate a developing restriction on the suction side. Depending on the system and operating point, a suction restriction may coincide with reduced suction pressure, degraded delivered flow, reduced discharge performance, abnormal noise/vibration associated with poor suction conditions, or other changes.

Motor current or power should be interpreted as a supporting signal rather than assumed to move in one universal direction. The direction depends on pump operating point, system curve, flow, control state, and equipment characteristics.

A diagnostic conclusion should therefore combine suction pressure, strainer differential pressure, flow, discharge pressure, operating state, and relevant equipment-condition measurements. Instrument quality and valve/configuration state should be checked before concluding that the restriction is physical.

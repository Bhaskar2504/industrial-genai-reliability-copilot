# Synthetic Engineering Note — Motor Current in Equipment Diagnosis

**Provenance:** Synthetic document written specifically for this portfolio project.

Motor current is a load-related electrical measurement and should not be treated as a universal signature of one mechanical failure. Its direction depends on equipment torque, process demand, control state, voltage, power factor, efficiency and the actual operating point.

For driven equipment such as pumps and fans, current should be interpreted with process flow, pressure, speed or damper/valve state and other condition indicators before drawing a mechanical conclusion.

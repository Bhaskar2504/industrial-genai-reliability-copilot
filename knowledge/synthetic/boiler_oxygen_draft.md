# Synthetic Engineering Note — Boiler Oxygen and Draft Relationship

**Provenance:** Synthetic document written specifically for this portfolio project.

Changes in flue-gas oxygen should be interpreted together with air flow, fuel condition, draft, leakage paths, load and measurement quality. Increased oxygen alone does not prove air ingress because combustion conditions and instrumentation can also change the reading.

When oxygen changes with draft burden or heat-recovery indicators, the relationship can support an engineering investigation into leakage, combustion balance or measurement quality.

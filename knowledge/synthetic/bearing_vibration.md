# Synthetic Engineering Note — Bearing Vibration Pattern

**Provenance:** Synthetic document written specifically for this portfolio project.

A change in bearing vibration should be interpreted with speed, load, vibration direction, frequency content, bearing temperature and recent maintenance context. A single increase in overall vibration does not uniquely identify unbalance, misalignment, looseness, rub or a bearing defect.

A useful diagnostic workflow compares the changing vibration pattern with operating state and other mechanical evidence, then recommends targeted inspection or spectral checks rather than declaring a root cause from one number.
